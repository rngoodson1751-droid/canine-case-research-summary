# Canine case A — preliminary retrospective summary

## Background
Owner-reported adult, spayed female Labrador-type mixed-breed dog. Exact age, birthday, weight, location and calendar dates are omitted from this public version.

## Reported course
The owner described several days of appetite loss, marked weakness and high fever. A high white blood cell count, low red blood count and elevated liver enzymes were relayed, but original numerical laboratory reports were unavailable. Concern about a possible liver lesion was relayed from the treating veterinarian, and an ultrasound study was undertaken. A soft subcutaneous chest lump was also owner-described without a tissue diagnosis.

The owner subsequently reported further loss of appetite and difficulty standing, with a decision for euthanasia to relieve suffering. Clinical documentation of the outcome and definitive diagnosis is pending.

## Evidence boundaries
All findings above are owner-reported or clinician findings relayed by the owner. They have not been verified against original veterinary records. No confirmed leukemia, cancer type, cause of death or relationship between the chest lump and acute illness is established.

Earlier chronic symptoms are withheld from this version because the underlying account has not been recovered sufficiently to verify them. Missing findings are unknown, not evidence of absence.

## Questions for future review
