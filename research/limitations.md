# Limitations and research disclaimer
- N=1 retrospective account with incomplete records and recall bias.
- Some clinical findings were relayed indirectly; the source reports have not been reviewed.
- Demographic and temporal detail is deliberately generalised to reduce identification.
- No comparison group, diagnostic validation or causal inference is possible from this summary.
- No estimate of prevalence, diagnostic probability or treatment effectiveness is supported.
- AI helped organise the text; it does not replace veterinary interpretation or source verification.
- This is educational documentation, not veterinary advice, a diagnosis, a treatment guide or a peer-reviewed publication.
- Do not use it as labelled ground truth for machine learning without source validation and appropriate permissions.
